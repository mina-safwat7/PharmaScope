Executive Summary

Auto-generated from the loaded dataset

This dataset covers a national cohort of 10,000 patients and 205,408 prescriptions, averaging 20.5 scripts per patient. The mean patient age is 47.0 years, concentrated in the 40–59 band.

Geographic coverage is complete across all 50 states with a notably tight spread — every state holds between 174 and 234 patients, averaging 200. This evenness indicates a deliberately balanced national sample.

Prescription supply is divided near-equally across five manufacturers (BioTech, CureWell, MedGen, MediHealth, PharmaCorp), each holding roughly 20% of volume. The average drug price is $6.77, putting the cohort's estimated supply value around $1.39M.
